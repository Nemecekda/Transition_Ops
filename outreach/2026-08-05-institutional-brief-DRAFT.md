## THE DRAFT — as written by pao-content, unedited

**SUBJECT:** Transition OPS — free tool for your referral list (Guard/Reserve depth, CVSO routing, and where it stops)

[Name],

Transition OPS (transitionops.org) is a free PWA built for transitioning service members and their families. No paid tier, no login required to use it, no ask attached to this email beyond what's below.

You advise transitions daily and you've seen plenty of apps that overpromise. This one is built to hold up under exactly the kind of scrutiny you'd apply before pointing a client at it. Here's what's in it, and — more to the point for a skeptical reader — what it deliberately does not do.

**GUARD/RESERVE — built for your caseload specifically:**
Points-statement reading (NGB 23B / DA Form 5016), the 50-point qualifying year, gray-area retirement, the 20-year letter's 90-day RC-SBP election window, TRICARE Reserve Select ending at SELRES departure, and the Chapter 1606 use-or-lose warning. This section is deeper than the rest of the app by design.

**Also in the app:**
- Critical windows — BDD (180–90 days out), GI Bill transfer rules while serving, SGLI→VGLI's 240-day no-questions window, the 180-day dental window, the one-year presumptive window, the one-year decision-review clock
- VA MATH — combined-ratings math on percentages the user enters
- VA PAY — 2026 COLA (2.8%, effective 1 DEC 2025), tax-free treatment, state-by-state treatment of military retirement pay
- Timeline built from the member's own separation date
- FIND YOUR VSO / CVSO — official locators (VA accredited-rep search, VA OGC accreditation search, NACVSO), plus a Wisconsin-specific Find My CVSO/TVSO link already built in
- Resources — VSO/support directory, Veterans Crisis Line (988, press 1), National Call Center for Homeless Veterans (877-424-3838)
- Career pathways and an AI resume drafter for civilian or USAJOBS applications, built from the member's own words, stores nothing
- AI Navigator (BETA) — answers only from the app's verified content and routes to a human when it can't

**What's new:** a daily scan against federal source feeds (VA, DoD, OPM, DOL-VETS) to flag policy changes. It detects; a person verifies before anything ships. Every benefits or policy figure in the app carries a dated verification record — currently 1 AUG 2026.

**What it does not do — this is the part that matters most for a referral:**
- Does not file claims and does not represent anyone. VSOs do that, free. The app routes to you.
- Does not predict a rating, a dollar figure, or a claim outcome. It explains process and stops there.
- No live job search yet. The DOL data-access request is still in the federal approval queue, so the app points to state job banks in the meantime.
- FIND YOUR VSO is a directory of official locators, not a search. It hands the user to the official tool — it doesn't return a name from a ZIP code.
- Not affiliated with VA or DoD. Educational information only.
- The Navigator is beta and stores nothing.
- No lender relationships, and it recommends no lender.

**One more thing, because it's the reason a verification record exists:** VET TEC 2.0 replaced the original program in June, including a change to how it charges GI Bill entitlement. We caught that our app carried the new terms correctly in one place while three older sections still described the retired program. We corrected all nine references the same day and logged it. That's what the verification process is built to catch — and why a referral to this app doesn't require taking our word for it.

**The ask:** run it against a case you're already advising this week and tell me where it's wrong. If it holds up, it's yours to point people at.

V/R,
Dean Nemecek
LTC, U.S. Army (Ret)
SPHR / SHRM-SCP

### ONE GAP, AND IT IS WORTH CLOSING BEFORE THIS SHIPS

**The draft says "stores nothing" twice. The app runs Google Analytics 4.**

Both instances are literally true as scoped — the Resume Drafter does not retain
resume content and the Navigator does not retain conversations. But two
unqualified "stores nothing" claims, in a brief to institutional gatekeepers,
create a broader privacy impression than the app supports. **This audience checks.
A CVSO who finds GA4 independently will discount everything else in the brief**,
and that is a bad trade for a sentence.

**The fix costs nothing, because the app is already honest about it in its own
words:**

> *"Your planning data — dates, ratings, checklists — stays on your device; we
> never see it. Anonymous usage analytics only. Optional email signup is used
> solely for update alerts and never sold."*

**Proposed addition to the "What it does not do" list — Dean's call:**

> - Planning data — dates, ratings, checklists — stays on the member's device; we
>   never see it. Anonymous usage analytics only, and the optional email signup
>   is used solely for update alerts and never sold.

This is not a correction of pao-content — the material I supplied did not include
the analytics fact, so the omission is mine. It also **strengthens** the brief:
it is the same move the VET TEC paragraph makes, and this audience rewards it.
