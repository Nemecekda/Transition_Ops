## THE DRAFT

**SUBJECT:** Free PWA tracks VA benefit deadlines — routes cases to your office, not around it

[Name],

Transition OPS is a free, no-login PWA (transitionops.org) built to help veterans track the deadlines that determine what they get from VA and DoD — and to send them to their county CVSO for anything that requires filing. It doesn't compete with your office. It's built to feed it.

**CRITICAL WINDOWS THE APP TRACKS**
- BDD claims — file 180 to 90 days before separation
- GI Bill transfer — must be initiated while still serving, via milConnect, with a 4-year ADSO
- SGLI to VGLI — 240-day no-exam conversion window
- The 180-day one-time dental window
- The one-year presumptive window under 38 CFR 3.307
- The one-year decision-review clock — miss it, and a later win pays from the new filing date, not the original

**VA MATH / VA PAY**
VA MATH runs VA's combined-ratings calculation on percentages the user enters — it demonstrates the math, it does not predict what VA will award. VA PAY covers the 2026 COLA (2.8%, effective 1 DEC 2025), the tax-free status of compensation, and state-by-state treatment of military retirement pay.

**IT ROUTES TO YOU**
FIND YOUR VSO/CVSO is a directory of official locators — VA's accredited-representative search, VA OGC's accreditation search, NACVSO, and a Wisconsin-specific Find My CVSO/TVSO link already built in. It does not take a ZIP code and hand back a person. It sends the member to you.

**NEW THIS MONTH — all verified live as of today**
- The National Call Center for Homeless Veterans (877-424-3838) is now listed in Resources next to the Veterans Crisis Line (988, press 1) — free, confidential, 24/7, connects directly to the nearest VA. Worth flagging to anyone on your caseload doing outreach or intake.
- Two OPM final rules take effect 2 SEP 2026 affecting federal RIF and probationary-termination procedures: RIF retention will lead on performance, with veterans' preference applied as points (5 for preference-eligibles with a compensable service-connected disability of 30% or higher, 3 for others) rather than a protected tier, and probationary appeals move from MSPB to a limited OPM process. Relevant if you carry veterans in federal service on your caseload. We are not making any claim about hiring preference — no source addresses that, so we don't either.
- We found and corrected a self-contradiction on VET TEC 2.0: three older app sections still described the prior program's GI Bill entitlement charge after the June update changed it. All nine references were corrected the same day and logged. The daily scan that caught it runs five federal sources — Federal Register feeds for VA, DoD, OPM and DOL-VETS, plus an eCFR title trip-wire that reports *that* a CFR title changed, never what changed. It flags; a person verifies before anything ships. The app's verification date, 1 AUG 2026, is shown on the page.

**ALSO IN THE APP** — a timeline built from the member's own separation date, career pathways with an AI resume drafter for civilian or USAJOBS applications built from the member's own words, TAX INTEL, DD214 guidance, a Guard/Reserve dashboard (points statements, the 50-point qualifying year, gray-area retirement, the 20-year letter's 90-day RC-SBP window, TRICARE Reserve Select, Chapter 1606 use-or-lose), and an AI Navigator (BETA) that answers only from verified content in the app.

**WHAT IT DOES NOT DO**
- Does not file claims and does not represent anyone. That's your office's work, and it's free. The app routes to you.
- Does not predict a rating, a dollar figure, or a claim outcome.
- No live job search yet — the DOL data-access request is still in the federal approval queue, so the app points to state job banks in the meantime.
- FIND YOUR VSO is a directory, not a search.
- Not affiliated with VA or DoD. Educational information only.
- The Navigator is BETA.
- No lender relationships, and it recommends no lender.

**PRIVACY** — in the app's own words: "Your planning data — dates, ratings, checklists — stays on your device; we never see it. Anonymous usage analytics only. Optional email signup is used solely for update alerts and never sold."
