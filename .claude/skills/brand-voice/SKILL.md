# BRAND VOICE — WRITING STANDARD

## VOICE
- Direct, professional, military-toned. Mission framing over commercial
  language ("serving transitioning members," not "growing our user base").
- Joint-force inclusive: ETS/EAOS/DOS together, MOS/AFSC/rating together.
  Never Army-only language for an all-branch audience.
- Lead with actionable intelligence and specific data. BLUF. No throat-
  clearing openers, no filler.
- NO performative content. Ever. No inspiration-bait, no engagement-bait,
  no holiday hot takes, no self-congratulation. If a post exists to be seen
  rather than to inform, kill it.
- Plain claims only: no invented statistics, no inflated past performance,
  no relationships that don't exist.

## CONVENTIONS
- Outreach sign-off: V/R, Dean Nemecek — with credentials where the audience
  warrants (LTC, U.S. Army Ret; SPHR/SHRM-SCP).
- Past performance references available: Cardinal IG, Trek Bicycle,
  UW-Green Bay, EVCO Plastics. Use only when relevant and accurate.
- Certifications: SDVOSB / VOSB / Wisconsin DVB. State plainly, no puffery.

## CHANNEL DISCIPLINE
- LinkedIn: employer-focused and B2B content lives here exclusively.
- Facebook: service-member/family-facing content only. Employer content
  does NOT cross-post to Facebook.
- Email outreach: one ask per message, receipts (specifics) up front,
  respect the reader's time.

## QUALITY GATE
Before delivery, verify: BLUF present · zero performative lines · joint-force
terms · every fact real · channel correct · sign-off correct.
