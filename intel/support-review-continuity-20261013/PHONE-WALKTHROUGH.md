# Five-minute phone walkthrough: follow through on career help

**Purpose:** Test whether a visitor can find Wisconsin work or training help, keep and recover a step, and turn a reported reply into a clear next action without coaching.

**Status:** Test plan only. No volunteers have been recruited or contacted; no sessions have been conducted. Record the candidate build before testing. Success targets below are not observed results.

## Setup

Use three volunteers, one fictional scenario each. Start at Home on a phone, in a dedicated browser profile containing only synthetic data. Allow normal storage; closing a private-browsing session may clear the plan and tests a different condition. Do not clear or replace real saved work.

No accounts, real resumes, member records, new analytics, recordings, external contacts, form submissions or paid AI requests. Any reply or document request below is invented, not actual Job Center requirements. Do not activate calls, emails or messages.

Record participant code A/B/C, date/time, build/URL, phone/browser, viewport if known, theme and starting storage state. Use the same build for all three. Keep notes anonymous and limited to navigation and synthetic choices.

## Read one scenario

| Code | Fictional situation | Place |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | You are leaving active service in a few months. You have logistics experience and want help with civilian work or training near where you plan to live. | Madison, Wisconsin |
| B | You are continuing Guard or Reserve service. You want help improving civilian job options while balancing ongoing service commitments. | Eau Claire, Wisconsin |
| C | You are a veteran working in a civilian job. You want to change careers and discuss local work or training options. | Milwaukee, Wisconsin |

The scenarios do not establish eligibility or available services.

## Five-minute script

Read tasks without naming menus, statuses or buttons. Do not point or suggest a route. Ask the volunteer to say what they expect to happen. If they ask for navigation help, first say: "Show me what you would try on your own." Record any later assistance.

| Target time | Read aloud | Observe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00-0:20 | "We are testing the app, not you. Use this fictional situation. Please do not enter your own information or contact anyone." Read their scenario. | Starting interpretation. |
| 0:20-1:45 | "Find Wisconsin help for work or training near that place. Keep one next step so you can return to it later." | Relevant help, deliberate saving, exact organization and step. Opening a link is not contacting someone. |
| 1:45-2:15 | "Close this tab. Open the app again and show me what you planned to do." | Same organization, action and relevant details recovered without help. |
| 2:15-3:40 | "For this pretend scenario, you spoke with the Job Center. They asked you to bring a resume and a list of jobs you want to discuss. Update your plan. Before keeping the next step, check the app's Wisconsin help information, return, and finish your review." | For A, B and C, observe one reference-check-and-return: does the same unsaved reply/action remain? Then check deliberate review and saving. No documents uploaded. |
| 3:40-4:20 | "Close and reopen the app again. Show me what you need to do now." | Updated step recovered, not the old contact step or another plan. |
| 4:20-5:00 | "In your own words, what happens next? Did the app send anything to the Job Center? What will still be here next time?" | Understands next action, actual sending and storage. Do not supply the answers. |

Let a slow phase continue until five minutes, then stop. Mark remaining phases **not attempted**. If you intervene to inspect a later phase, record it and classify dependent results as assisted. Access failure is not a usability pass.

## Targets and interpretation

**Targets, not claims:** Each volunteer finishes without navigation help, recovers the correct step twice, and explains what to do next. They distinguish recording a reply from sending a message, and a proposed action from enrollment or employment. Their storage explanation matches the tested build: moving between app pages retains an unfinished review; refreshing or closing the tab clears it until explicitly saved. The reference detour is within the app; the later close/reopen follows saving.

Lost/replaced work, the wrong organization, unintended sending or misleading saved/sent confirmation warrants reproduction even once. Prioritize repeated hesitation or wrong turns. Completing the route without understanding the next action is not a comprehension pass.

Report at most three findings: observed event, affected task, proposed correction and cheap retest. Keep assisted, incomplete and unattempted results visible. These are three small qualitative sessions, not proof of retention, population success, accessibility or universal ease. Keep staff walkthroughs and synthetic automated checks separate. Reuse the same scenario and conditions when retesting; disclose differences.
