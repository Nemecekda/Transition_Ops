## Caseworker handout

### Find one role to discuss, even without a finished resume

Exploring a new role without a finished resume? Open [Career in Transition OPS](https://transitionops.org/?tool=pathway) and select **Continue without a resume**. Choose a field and role, select **Review and save**, then **Use for this visit**. Open **Search jobs by area**, enter where you want to work, and use a job-site search link.

The links start a search; they do not confirm that a job is available or that you qualify. Bring one posting to your next conversation with your caseworker or career adviser. Point to one requirement you would like help comparing with your experience.
