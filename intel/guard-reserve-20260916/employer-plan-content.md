## Recognition boundaries

Use **Find ESGR pledge signers** for https://maps.esgrevents.mil/ and **Recognize a supportive supervisor** for https://www.esgr.mil/Service-Members-Family/Nominate-your-Employer . Keep the verified pledge boundary: **A signed pledge does not establish an award, an open job or a hiring guarantee.** Official search results depend on their selected signing-date range. Verify openings on the employer's current careers page; do not describe the pledge map as a job board.

The app helps a member organize a manually chosen opportunity. It does not establish an ESGR partnership, endorse employers, prove workplace policy compliance, submit a job application, or confirm receipt by an employer.
