# Guard and Reserve career support: implementation content

Prepared September 16, 2026. Owned content packet only. No app edits, shared-log edits, company endorsements or outreach. Apply brand-voice and policy-verification standards. This is proposed preview copy and source evidence, not release approval.

## Recommended compact Home copy

Title: **Your career, throughout your service**

Supporting text: **Build your civilian career while you serve. Keep a target role, your next step and the questions you want help answering.**

Primary action: **Work on my career plan**

Primary helper: **Compare your experience with a role's requirements and choose your next action.**

This points to the existing Career Gap worksheet and preparation summary. Do not promise placement, credential approval, eligibility or employment outcomes. Support a long-serving member's next career move without requiring an imminent separation date.

## Employer recognition route

Section title: **Employers that pledged support**

Body: **An ESGR Statement of Support records an employer's pledge to support Guard and Reserve employees. Use it as a starting point, then check the employer's current jobs and military-leave policies.**

Boundary: **A signed pledge does not establish an award, an open job or a hiring guarantee.**

Action: **Find ESGR pledge signers**

Source/fallback action: **About the ESGR pledge** -> https://www.esgr.mil/Employers/Statement-of-Support

Optional secondary recognition action: **Recognize a supportive supervisor** -> https://www.esgr.mil/Service-Members-Family/Nominate-your-Employer

Helper: **The Patriot Award recognizes supervisors nominated for supporting Guard and Reserve employees.** Keep the official page responsible for nomination details; do not reproduce the application or imply submission from the app.

The official Statement page links to https://maps.esgrevents.mil/ . Root verified the public map and executed a company-name search successfully in Chrome. Use that exact destination for **Find ESGR pledge signers**. Optional help: **Results depend on the selected signing-date range. Check current jobs and workplace policies with the employer.** Do not substitute scraped company names or a static named-employer carousel. No named employer has been verified in this packet.

Keep three concepts separate in labels and data:

- **Statement of Support signed**: a pledge, with a signature date where officially available.
- **ESGR award recipient**: an independently sourced award name and year, if ever implemented.
- **Current job opening**: a current employer job posting, separately verified for existence and requirements.

Do not call a signatory "ESGR-certified," "approved employer," "vetted workplace," or an award winner without the corresponding evidence. A pledge date is not a last-verified hiring date. No signatory expiry, required renewal interval or completeness guarantee was verified. Absence from the directory must not become an adverse rating.

## Support-office copy

Keep this as one secondary disclosure, not a directory occupying the Home screen.

Disclosure: **Find career and family support**

Helper: **Find an office for your question, then add its public contact reference to your plan.**

Links:

| Link label | Exact URL | Short context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find a Family Assistance Center | https://www.militaryonesource.mil/resources/tools/national-guard-family-program-lookup/ | Find local referral support across military components. |
| Army Reserve Family Programs | https://www.usar.army.mil/arfp/ | Find Army Reserve family support contacts. |
| Military OneSource | https://www.militaryonesource.mil/resources/millife-guides/eligibility-for-military-onesource-services/ | Serving Guard and Reserve members and eligible immediate families can get support regardless of activation status. |
| Wisconsin Service Member Support | https://dma.wi.gov/service-member-support-division/ | Connect with Wisconsin's service member and family support team. |

Show the Wisconsin link as a clearly labeled state example or when the member selects Wisconsin, not as the national default. Do not label all offices J9. Family Assistance Centers offer referrals across components; federal reservists also have component-specific networks. Referral access does not mean eligibility for every referred program. The Army Reserve link must not be presented as the family-program office for every Reserve branch.

Optional office helper in preparation fields: **Office names vary by state and component. Use the name and public contact reference published by that office.**

Avoid introducing state benefits, legal determinations, deadlines, financial figures or an implied government connection to Transition OPS.

## Implementation boundaries

The lifelong-career framing is a product recommendation, not a government program promise. Reuse the existing career worksheet and member-reported follow-up. No broad new profile, mandatory support workflow, named employer dataset, automatic source import, or employer scoring is proposed. Keep recognition and actual openings separate; use the official recognition route and an employer's own current careers page for different questions.
