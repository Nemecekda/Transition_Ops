# Guard and Reserve: recurring value and support routing

Research only, September 16, 2026. No app changes or shared verification-log edits. Sources below were read through direct web retrieval (rung 1) on this date unless an access barrier is explicitly recorded. Findings are narrow source claims, not a release approval. Formal member-impact entries belong with verification-log records before any future app copy ships.

## Answer and boundaries

State Guard family/support offices are sensible partners and referral destinations. "J9" is an organizational label, not a universal program name or the only support channel for federal Reserve members. Wisconsin publicly uses Service Member Support Division; Colorado uses Joint Resiliency Directorate (J9). Route by component, location, and the specific problem; show the office's published name.

Do not create a false exclusion: the national Family Assistance Center locator says its referral services support geographically dispersed families and retirees from all military components. A federal reservist can therefore have a useful local FAC connection even though the Army Reserve has its own Family Programs network. Referral access does not establish eligibility for every program to which an office refers someone.

The strategy recommendation is an inference: use a small, member-maintained list of unresolved actions and trusted support contacts across recurring military/civilian changes. A static state-office directory alone provides limited reason to return. No retention or demand claim has been measured.

## Primary source records

**GR-01 | CONFIRMED | Wisconsin example.** Wisconsin DMA's Service Member Support Division connects service members, veterans and families with federal, state and community resources. It lists information/referral and nonmedical administrative case management across financial, employment, TRICARE and ID/DEERS matters. Its public contact is 1-800-292-9464, option 1, and its published mailbox includes J9. The page does not make every listed benefit universally available. [Wisconsin DMA](https://dma.wi.gov/service-member-support-division/). Read service mission, readiness, essential-services and contact sections; did not audit individual linked grants.

**GR-02 | CONFIRMED | National locator and scope.** The locator describes state/territory family-program staff and FAC referral support for geographically dispersed families and retirees from all military components. [Military OneSource National Guard Family Program Lookup](https://www.militaryonesource.mil/resources/tools/national-guard-family-program-lookup/). Read entire descriptive page. The interactive state search was not tested; do not claim every returned contact was verified.

**GR-03 | CONFIRMED | Naming varies.** Colorado's published J9 title is Joint Resiliency Directorate; it describes support to Colorado Guard members and families. This is a naming example, not proof of universal eligibility. [Colorado National Guard J9](https://co.ng.mil/J9/). Read title, mission and support introduction.

**GR-04 | CONFIRMED | Federal Reserve route.** Army Reserve Family Programs supports Soldiers and families assigned to the command, coordinates unit/community resources and provides a family-crisis assistance gateway. Its page links directors/coordinators and regional points of contact. [Army Reserve Family Programs](https://www.usar.army.mil/arfp/). Read main program and contact content. This establishes an Army Reserve route, not a single office for all federal Reserve branches; Navy, Marine Corps, Air Force and Coast Guard routing needs branch-specific verification before directory implementation.

**GR-05 | CONFIRMED | Military OneSource scope.** Serving National Guard and Reserve members have access regardless of activation status; immediate family members are also listed. Finances, relationships and other personal-life support are covered. Separate rules apply to former members, extended family and particular subprograms; do not extend this to every veteran indefinitely. Eligibility questions can go to Military OneSource at 800-342-9647. [Eligibility page](https://www.militaryonesource.mil/resources/millife-guides/eligibility-for-military-onesource-services/). Read eligibility, temporary/partial access and service overview. No time-limit or subprogram entitlement is proposed here.

**GR-06 | CONFIRMED | Health coverage changes.** TRICARE states that Guard/Reserve health-plan options depend on the sponsor's military status, which may change repeatedly, and directs members to milConnect to verify eligibility. IRR members are distinguished from other Reserve categories. [TRICARE Guard and Reserve](https://www.tricare.mil/reserve), page updated March 27, 2026. Read substantive eligibility/status sections. Do not translate "Reserve" into automatic TRICARE Reserve Select, TAMP or continuous coverage eligibility.

**GR-07 | CONFIRMED, narrowly scoped | Yellow Ribbon.** Wisconsin's official support page describes YRRP as supporting Guard/Reserve members and families throughout the deployment cycle. The program's FAQ directs event/course questions to YRRP coordinators and event-specific points of contact, and explains registration approval and official event-dashboard completion. [YRRP FAQ](https://www.yellowribbon.mil/PageHelp/Frequently_Asked_Questions.htm). Read full FAQ. The program homepage returned no extractable content. No universal event admission, funded travel, guest eligibility or attendance requirement has been established. Do not confuse this reintegration program with VA's tuition Yellow Ribbon Program.

## Five recurring member jobs supported by read sources

These are condition-triggered concepts, not separation-countdown reminders. ACT AVAILABLE NOW means the official inquiry/check is available, not that an individual benefit or event is approved. Proposed surface: Guard/Reserve module; no new Navigator token is proposed or claimed live. Expires: NONE identified; reverify sources before implementation.

1. **When orders or duty status change: confirm health coverage.** Population: Guard/Reserve members and families affected by that status change. Act: "Use milConnect to verify your TRICARE eligibility when your military status changes." App role: record the question still unresolved and next official contact; do not calculate eligibility from a checkbox. This does not establish coverage for every IRR member or every Guard/Reserve duty category. Potential cost addressed: a wrong coverage assumption; exact financial/window effects were not assessed. Source GR-06.
2. **When a family/support issue remains unresolved: reach the right office and follow up.** Population: Guard/Reserve families seeking referrals; include retirees in the FAC route where its source explicitly does. Act: "Use the National Guard Family Program Lookup to find a Family Assistance Center, or use Army Reserve Family Programs' coordinator list for an Army Reserve request." App role: one question, public office reference, member-reported contact status and next follow-up. This does not promise every referred service to every caller. No fixed phase or measurable financial cost is established. Sources GR-01/02/04.
3. **When household finances or relationships need attention: prepare for a support consultation.** Population: serving Guard/Reserve members and eligible immediate families, regardless of activation status. Act: "Contact Military OneSource at 800-342-9647 for help with your financial or family-support question and confirmation of applicable services." App role: keep an optional agenda and next action; avoid collecting case narratives. This does not extend serving-member eligibility indefinitely to separated members or all extended family. Condition-triggered; no financial result promised. Source GR-05.
4. **During a deployment/reintegration cycle: resolve Yellow Ribbon event questions.** Population: Guard/Reserve members/families considering or registered for a relevant event. Act: "Contact the point of contact on your Yellow Ribbon event's details page about participation or registration questions." App role: member-entered event reference, questions and follow-up; official attendance/completion stays with the program. This does not apply to the VA tuition program or guarantee a funded place. Condition-triggered; no universal deadline. Source GR-07.

5. **When military service affects civilian employment: prepare a support request.** Population: Guard/Reserve members with service-connected civilian-employment problems. Act: "Contact DOL VETS through its USERRA page for help with your civilian-employment issue." App role: track the question and public contact reference, and remind the member to retain source documents outside the app. This does not determine whether a particular service period, employer or reemployment claim qualifies for protection. Condition-triggered; the help request addresses a potential wrong employment action, with no promised recovery. Source GR-09. ESGR-specific service details remain pending the separate source read.

## Safe priorities

First, test the already-developed counselor preparation pattern with Guard/Reserve users: an issue, the correct office, the unanswered question and next follow-up. Second, add a narrowly verified duty-status/coverage checkpoint. Yellow Ribbon can become an event-specific use of the same pattern. Military OneSource offers a cross-component referral route; it should not become a promise of every subprogram.

A Wisconsin support-office conversation could validate which unresolved requests recur and which published routing labels members recognize. This is a proposed research conversation, not authorized outreach or a claimed partnership. Measure whether members resolve their chosen action and return when a new issue arises; do not manufacture a daily engagement requirement.
