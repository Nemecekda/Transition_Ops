## Minimum design

1. **Identity continuity:** fix the existing Guard Home planning action to open the relevant screen without changing `tops_user_status` to active. Existing `guard` is a combined legacy value, not evidence of Army Guard, Air Guard or a particular federal Reserve component. Restore an existing saved branch only after validating its known values; never infer Guard versus Reserve from branch alone. An optional component choice must allow unknown and remain separate from present duty status. No new orders/activation-date intake or eligibility engine.
2. **One career next action:** use the existing Gap worksheet/counselor summary as the work destination. Member can continue a saved goal, identify one requirement, or update one followup. Do not create a second plan, top-level menu, generic feed, artificial deadline, readiness score or auto-completion. A blank worksheet should produce an honest starter action; saved data does not imply a qualification or unresolved legal requirement.
3. **Support office preparation:** place a small national/Wisconsin choice with the current office guidance. Use S2-verified published titles and public references, with a reason to contact each. Wisconsin's Service Member Support Division should not be renamed a universal J9. National FAC referral access can serve multiple components per the official source; do not incorrectly exclude federal reservists. Army Reserve Family Programs is an Army Reserve route, not an all-Reserve office. Military OneSource may supply a verified broader fallback. Selection of Wisconsin is explicit and distinct from tax residence, jobs-search state and Guard affiliation.
4. **Reuse the summary:** an explicit "Prepare my question" action can open the existing worksheet and offer the selected public office reference. Preserve member-entered goals/questions/followup; never overwrite silently. Default contact status remains unknown. Opening an office link does not mean contacted, receipt acknowledged, referred, case opened or official completion. No note is sent to an office.

## ESGR recognition boundary

User authorization covers a useful recognition feature in the preview. Exact claims still need a readable current official source: the existing `source-review.md` GR-08 reports ESGR retrieval failures, so S2/root must resolve source access before promoting specific claims. This does not block independent identity/worksheet work.

Safe initial scope after verification is an explanation of what the **Statement of Support** means, an official program link, and the applicable official action. Distinguish signing a statement from an ESGR award, current job openings, legal compliance certification, employer vetting or demonstrated outcomes. Do not call a signer a guaranteed good employer, preferred employer, government-endorsed employer, or promised source of employment. Do not conflate Statement of Support with Patriot Award, Freedom Award, or another recognition category.

If a specific employer is shown, record its exact legal/display identity, official evidence of the stated recognition, recognition type/date and source-check date. An outdated press release cannot prove current hiring or current membership. Avoid ranking or stale badges; do not add logos, scraped lists, contact forms, commercial referrals or paid offerings as a side effect. Existing S2-vetting's commercial-resource lane applies to an affirmative employer/service recommendation; the user's request to recognize official participation is not blanket approval to endorse every participant. The minimal official-program route requires no new broad employer-vetting skill.

- Public links are user-initiated external navigation, not integrations. No source auto-fetch, automatic submission, employer outreach or office outreach. Local shared-device visibility remains relevant; avoid universal privacy claims.
- Existing substantive Guard advice flagged by S3 is not made verified by this feature. Do not newly promote broad funding, benefit, timing or eligibility statements without S2 resolution.
