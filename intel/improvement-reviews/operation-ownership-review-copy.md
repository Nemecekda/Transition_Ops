## Operation Ownership

Veteran business ownership in Greater Milwaukee

Operation Ownership describes itself as a long-term economic development and veteran leadership initiative connecting veterans with business ownership opportunities as existing owners plan for succession. Led by a group of veteran business owners, the initiative aims to support veterans and the local economy by helping connect prospective veteran owners with businesses seeking a future successor.
