## Cross-cutting needs outside this denominator

The WDVA overview is not the whole member journey. Current and former Guard/Reserve members also need DMA/unit support, return-to-civilian-work guidance, healthcare transitions and retirement information. This slice separately verifies DMA Service Member Support (11), Retiree Activities Office (12), DOL USERRA (USERRA), an optional bounded ESGR referral (ESGR), and the national VA state-office directory (NATIONAL). Detailed return-from-orders healthcare, federal Reserve education and retirement rules remain open in `source-coverage.md`. Likewise, the 12-topic denominator does not cover every DHS/DWD/DOR/DSPS program or local food, energy, transport, childcare and housing-assistance program.

This order is an editorial assessment of potential cost and harm, not a measured ranking of demand or outcomes. Each next loop should add one verified useful action, not another large catalog.

1. **Family-safe housing and essential-cost help.** Finish the route for a veteran with dependents, a serving member or a prospective mover who cannot use VHRP housing. Acceptance: a synthetic family can identify an appropriate official next contact without a promise of a bed, cash or veteran eligibility.
2. **Education funding and residency coordination.** Verify the remaining education pathways and coordination questions for a returning veteran, a Wisconsin Guard member and a member of another Reserve component. Acceptance: each can identify the correct deciding office/school and what to confirm before enrolling or paying, without conflating state and federal GI Bills or assuming a move qualifies them.
3. **Survivor and long-term-care next steps.** Verify Wisconsin burial/military-honors and Veterans Homes contact/application routes, with distinct veteran and family conditions. Acceptance: a family can reach the right office for the actual need without mistaking temporary housing for long-term care or assuming a survivor award.
