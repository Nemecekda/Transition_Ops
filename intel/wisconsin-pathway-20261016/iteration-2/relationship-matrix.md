# Program relationship matrix: Wisconsin work and SkillBridge

Checked **October 16, 2026, America/Chicago**. Evidence-only companion to `runtime-copy.json` and `verification-log-append.md`. These are the combinations relevant to this iteration, not all public benefits, employer incentives or military transition rules. Sources were read directly; SkillBridge HTML needed root browser escalation. No personal eligibility is decided.

**Disposition key:** CONFIRMED means only the stated rule; CONDITIONAL means the source explicitly supports the combination subject to its rules; NOT ESTABLISHED means this research does not support a positive or negative universal claim. A later paid hire is separate from work performed during SkillBridge. A verified program pair is not evidence that a particular member, employer or expense qualifies under both.
