## 1. New VA OJT summary: avoid assuming prior active duty

Old: `After active duty, eligible trainees may receive GI Bill support while earning wages in a VA-approved apprenticeship or on-the-job training program.`

New: `Eligible trainees who are not on active duty may receive GI Bill support while earning wages in a VA-approved apprenticeship or on-the-job training program.`

Retain boundary: `VA must approve the training program and your benefit eligibility. Active-duty members cannot receive this GI Bill OJT benefit; spouses using transferred benefits while the member is active duty are also excluded.`

This does not imply a drilling Reservist or eligible dependent must first have served on active duty. JSON and verification append now use this exact new sentence.
