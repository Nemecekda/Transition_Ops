# Wisconsin government support and employer-recognition review

Checked **October 16, 2026, America/Chicago**. This iteration uses current primary web searches, direct official-page reads, and root browser escalation. It is a bounded referral review, not a claim that every Wisconsin benefit, nonprofit, employer or county service is covered. Nonprofit verification is separately owned by `wi_loop_coverage`; state referral pages are not substitutes for IRS status, claims accreditation, current services or capacity checks.

## Findings that change the member experience

1. **Turn recognition into a useful job-search route.** DWD's **Vets Ready Employer Initiative** recognizes veteran support. Its visible roster is headed **2025 winners**, independently corroborated by DWD's dated recipient release. Root tested the public JCW path: Job Search → Advanced Search → Vets Ready Employers → All Vets Ready Employers. Results had the recognition icon without login. The base link does not preselect the filter; no transient count or company vacancy is promoted as durable evidence. This re-runs the iteration-1 DECLINE: award administration is still not a member task, but the verified job-search asset is now useful.
2. **Make help match the need.** County/tribal offices handle benefits questions; Job Centers handle employment assessment; VA's representative locator handles accredited claims representation. DVOP has targeted eligibility and LVER is an employer-facing route. The existing VSO introduction overgeneralizes free service and accreditation; exact bounded replacements are in `adjacent-claims-copy.md`.
3. **Close a household-support gap.** VA SSVF is a family housing-stability route for qualifying low-income veteran households facing homelessness, distinct from a veteran-only residential bed. The public directory route is verified, while provider capacity remains unknown. WDVA's Women Veterans Program provides a separate official support/connection contact. These are conditional details, not new first-screen hurdles.

## Northern and rural routing boundary

The WDVA county/tribal locator is a **statewide discovery route**, including people looking toward northern or rural Wisconsin. The official tribal-office page also publishes northern tribal-office contacts. Reuse this route for the county or tribal office relevant to the member's Wisconsin location or intended move; do not substitute a Milwaukee provider as if its service area were statewide. An office can review the actual benefits question and local referral need, but the directory is not a guarantee of a specific program's geography, intake or eligibility.

For household housing instability, the official **VA SSVF provider directory route** is the appropriate national-to-local lookup. The FY26 workbook is linked by VA; this packet does not establish which provider currently covers a northern county or has intake capacity. The receiving provider must confirm its actual service area. The absence of a named rural nonprofit in a search is not evidence that no provider exists.

No new regional card or copied roster is required. The existing county/tribal route and conditional SSVF lookup are the useful fallback while organization-specific verification continues.
