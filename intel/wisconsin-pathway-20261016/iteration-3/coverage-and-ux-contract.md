## Distinctions that must survive the UI

| Concept | What it establishes | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| County/tribal Veterans Service Office | An official local starting point for benefits questions and applications, within the recorded source scope | That a visitor is eligible or has applied |
| Veterans service organization | An organization or membership network | That every chapter, volunteer or staff member is an accredited claims representative |
| VA accreditation | The status and scope of the exact organization/representative in the current official record | A disability outcome or guarantee of immediate help |
| Nonprofit/tax-exempt status | Legal identity and the recorded IRS classification | Quality, capacity, free service, universal eligibility or every advertised service |
| Employer recognition | The issuer's named award/level in the stated year | Current openings, job fit, reserved placement, SkillBridge/VA training approval or a guarantee of workplace treatment |
| Veteran-owned business certification | Ownership/procurement status within the certifier's rules | Veteran hiring quality or a vacancy |
| VBS cohort membership | Dean's stated relationship with the named company | Any government award, approved placement or preferential access |

Avoid a single "verified" badge that collapses these meanings. Do not label a directory "Military Ready employers" until the exact program name and issuer are established.
