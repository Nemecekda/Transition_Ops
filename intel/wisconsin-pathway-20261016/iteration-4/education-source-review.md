# Education and county housing source review — 2026-10-16

Scope: agency process and referral guidance, not a complete adjudication of Wisconsin education entitlement. Source visits were public and read-only. No account, application, member record, provider message or paid API was used.

## Cleared boundaries

- [WDVA Wisconsin GI Bill](https://dva.wi.gov/benefits/education/wi-gi-bill/): agency certification and school student determination are separate. MyWisVets/WDVA2030 and school WDVA2029 are the concrete sequence. The school decides residency for tuition. Reuse current route; do not add a numerical residency assertion.
- [UW campus veterans resources](https://www.wisconsin.edu/veteran-military-connected-student-resources/): official campus-office links and pre-enrollment coordination. [WTCS veterans services](https://mywtcs.wtcsystem.edu/educational-services/student-services/veterans-services/): public-system contact-list route, parent page read. No named official copied from the inaccessible contact PDF.
- [VA education eligibility](https://www.va.gov/education/eligibility/) and [GI Bill contact](https://www.benefits.va.gov/gibill/contact_us.asp): multiple service/family routes and benefit-choice consequences warrant a direct deciding-office link. No election made by the app, no promise of additional entitlement or reversible choice.
- [Selected Reserve GI Bill](https://www.va.gov/education/about-gi-bill-benefits/montgomery-selected-reserve/): unit DD2384-1 and approved-program check are useful before enrollment. Do not reduce qualification to Reserve membership alone or imply that every former Reservist remains eligible.
- [Wisconsin Guard tuition](https://dma.wi.gov/wisconsin-national-guard-tuition-grant/) and [FAQ](https://dma.wi.gov/wisconsin-national-guard-tuition-grant-faqs/): current online term application, administrator, program conditions and school payee. No award calculation, exact deadline or all-members claim is added. Other Reserve and family statuses are distinct.
- [HEAB program](https://heab.state.wi.us/features/vets.html), [November2025 application](https://heab.state.wi.us/files/features/wiveterans.pdf), [Q&A](https://heab.state.wi.us/files/features/wivets-qanda.pdf): separate private nonprofit grant, participating-school check, WDVA certification, student/school process, agency verification and school payment. Federal tuition funding affects the state award. No dollars, universal stack, personal document ingestion or school list copied into app.
- [VetEd page](https://dva.wi.gov/benefits/education/vet-ed-reimbursement-grant/) and [May2025 fact sheet](https://dva.wi.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/WDVA_2468-VetEd-Fact-Sheet.pdf): conditional reimbursement after successful study, no prior bachelor's degree, WDVA/CVSO-TVSO help and WDVA2200 route are common supported facts. Exact interplay with other benefits is not cleared; users get the deciding office before relying on reimbursement.
- [VORP](https://dva.wi.gov/services/mental-health-and-emergency-services/veterans-outreach-and-recovery/): serves people who have served, including current Guard/Reserve; helps connect housing/utilities and other services. Current regional contacts include explicit supervisor cover for vacancies. Public text read directly; county-map image read by root browser and preserved as vorp-county-map-browser.png. No staff capacity, funding award or bed claim.
- [WDVA county/tribal office page](https://dva.wi.gov/resources/find-my-cvso-tvso/) links [the locator](https://wicvso.org/locate-your-cvso-tvso/), which was directly readable with county and tribal entries. Reuse the existing cleared government referral, not new blanket vetting or an accreditation claim about every listed person.

## Data handling and narrow interpretation

No agency documents are uploaded to Transition OPS. Forms mentioned here may request sensitive identifiers on their official sites; the app links out and does not collect them. County/state selections remain preferences, not verified residence. An agency visit does not mark a task as applied, eligible, contacted or completed.

VORP is a local connection route, not an independently verified housing entitlement for every family member. Keep the already cleared SSVF/CVI household pathway and VHRP occupancy limits. A current directory establishes where to inquire, not whether service is available today.
