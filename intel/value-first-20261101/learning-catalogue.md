## Nine additions  -  bounded copy for implementation

### 1. Microsoft Learn: Cloud concepts

- URL: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/paths/microsoft-azure-fundamentals-describe-cloud-concepts/
- Suggested ID: `microsoft-cloud-concepts`; family: IT support, help desk, cloud and technical operations.
- What: Learn cloud service models and shared responsibility. Training completion does not award Azure certification.
- Access: Free learning content; no account needed to read. A free profile tracks progress. Certification exams and optional cloud services have separate terms.
- Practice: Explain the difference between IaaS, PaaS and SaaS using one workplace example.
- Evidence: exact introductory path and [Learn FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/support/faq). The path mentions Azure account choices separately. Recommend its conceptual modules without implying paid cloud use or a trial is necessary/free indefinitely.

### 2. HubSpot Academy: Digital marketing

- URL: https://academy.hubspot.com/courses/digital-marketing
- Suggested ID: `hubspot-digital-marketing`; family: marketing, communications, content and small-business outreach.
- What: Learn introductory content, search, email and social marketing. The course offers a HubSpot course certification after its requirements; it is not a professional license.
- Access: Free course and HubSpot Academy account. Complete the course requirements for its credential.
- Practice: Draft a short audience, goal and channel plan for a fictional service.
- Evidence: exact page identifies this specific offering as a free certification course and includes a certification. It is a genuine provider-issued course credential, not merely free exam preparation. Do not generalize to HubSpot software certifications or promise access to paid HubSpot products. The separately checked [Inbound Sales course](https://academy.hubspot.com/courses/inbound-sales) is another specific free certification option if a later curated swap is needed; it is not a fifteenth entry in this packet.

### 3. IBM SkillsBuild: Cybersecurity fundamentals

- URL: https://skillsbuild.org/digital-credentials  -  choose **Cybersecurity Fundamentals**.
- Suggested ID: `ibm-cybersecurity`; family: cybersecurity and IT support foundations.
- What: Explore foundational threats, risk and security practices. This learning badge is not a professional security certification.
- Access: Free learning with a free SkillsBuild account. The adult learner route is for ages 18 and over; check the selected badge's requirements.
- Practice: Write a short checklist for spotting a phishing message, using a made-up example.
- Evidence: [credential catalogue](https://skillsbuild.org/digital-credentials) labels this credential for all learners; [adult learner page](https://skillsbuild.org/adult-learners) confirms free online learning; [FAQ](https://skillsbuild.org/frequently-asked-questions) confirms adult route, registration and credential conditions. The public catalogue is the verified destination; a short start-learning redirect timed out during research, so no authenticated course access was claimed. Do not substitute partner-only certificate programs.

### 4. OSHA workplace safety eTools

- URL: https://www.osha.gov/apps-and-etools (`/etools` redirects here).
- Suggested ID: `osha-safety-guidance`; family: trades, HVAC/maintenance, manufacturing, construction and warehouse safety awareness.
- What: Explore public workplace hazard and safety guidance. This is not OSHA 10/30 training, an OSHA card or a trade license.
- Access: Free public guidance; no account needed to read. Employer-required training and safe work procedures still apply.
- Practice: Choose one relevant hazard and write questions to ask a supervisor before doing the work.
- Evidence: official page describes interactive safety/health education tools and lists topics including construction, lockout/tagout and machine guarding. This recommendation is reference learning, not qualification to perform hazardous work or a compliance determination.

### 5. OpenLearn: Bookkeeping and accounting

- URL: https://www.open.edu/openlearn/money-business/introduction-bookkeeping-and-accounting
- Suggested ID: `openlearn-bookkeeping`; family: finance, bookkeeping, administration and business operations.
- What: Learn double-entry bookkeeping, ledger accounts and trial balances with UK examples. This does not teach U.S. tax rules or award an accounting license.
- Access: Free course; read without an account. A free account is needed for activities, progress and a Statement of Participation; no formal academic credit.
- Practice: Record three fictional transactions in a simple ledger and check the balances.
- Evidence: exact course page confirms its content and free/no-signup reading; [OpenLearn FAQ](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/about-openlearn/frequently-asked-questions-on-openlearn) distinguishes completion records from formal credit. Parent also independently verified this course.

Parent supplied corroborating 2025 fact-sheet/brochure evidence. Do not claim first-responder licensure, hiring eligibility, college credit or continuing-education acceptance by a profession.
