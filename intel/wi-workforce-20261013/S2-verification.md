## Sources and bounded findings

- [DWD 8.6 Supportive Services](https://dwd.wisconsin.gov/wioa/policy/08/08.6.htm): 8.6.2 (effective April 12, 2019) lists tools, work clothing/equipment and employment/training-related certification/license fees among possible services. 8.6.3 (effective December 1, 2024) requires need, a career/training-service connection and inability to obtain timely support elsewhere. 8.6.6 (effective October 10, 2018) leaves offerings and limits to local policy and forbids reimbursement without prior approval. This supports asking, not a funding promise.
- [DWD 8.2 Eligibility](https://dwd.wisconsin.gov/wioa/policy/08/08.2.htm): 8.2.1 and 8.2.2 effective October 1, 2024. Every applicant needs an eligibility determination; funding is finite. Dislocated Worker Category 3 requires qualifying separation documentation and an other-than-dishonorable discharge or expectation; Selective Service requirements apply when applicable. State/county residence cannot be added as program eligibility criteria. Safe practical wording: ask before a move or separation; confirm which office can serve the case.
- [Wisconsin ETPL search](https://dwd.wisconsin.gov/etpl/home/programsearch): read October 13; no revision/effective date displayed. Career-planner approval is required for training funds; starting training first is not reimbursable. Assistance/type limits may apply. Listing does not establish an individual's funding approval. [DWD 7.2](https://dwd.wisconsin.gov/wioa/policy/07/07.2.htm), effective January 20, 2020, explains the list's role for ITA-funded training, not all supportive services.
- [Wisconsin Workforce Services Directory](https://wisconsinjobcenter.org/directory/): read October 13; no revision/effective date displayed. County-based service finder, Adult and Dislocated Worker referrals, veteran employment services. Official origin corroborated by [DWD Employment and Training Contacts](https://dwd.wisconsin.gov/det/contact.htm), which links this directory and lists 888-258-9966.

## Suggested compact app copy

**Help with Wisconsin job costs**

Need tools, work gear or training? A Wisconsin Job Center can assess whether WIOA workforce funding can help. Support depends on eligibility, your career or training plan, local rules and available funding.

**Before you spend**

Ask your career planner what can be approved, which documents are needed and how payment works. Get approval before buying equipment or starting training. You can ask before separating or moving to Wisconsin.

Primary link: **Find a Wisconsin Job Center**. Secondary link: **Search training programs**. ETPL note: **A listed program is not a funding approval.**
