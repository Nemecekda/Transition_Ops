Employer review brief

Transition Ops for an ERG, BRG, or military-connected employee group

A client-neutral starting point for organizations reviewing a free public transition-planning resource for their military-connected community.

Bottom line

Transition Ops is publicly accessible at no cost. Use is optional. An organization can review the public site and, after its own approval, place one ordinary direct link in an existing resource area.

Review Transition Ops

What the resource provides

Transition Ops is a public planning resource for transitioning service members, veterans, Guard and Reserve members, military spouses, caregivers, and families. It brings transition timelines, benefits resources, career tools, and practical checklists into one place.

Recommended first step

  1. Review this brief and the public Transition Ops site.
  2. Route the resource through the organization's existing review process.
  3. Publish only the direct public link after internal approval.
  4. Keep employer policy and contacts on employer-managed systems.

This static package does not require

  • A client tenant, software installation, member account, SSO, or HRIS connection.
  • An ERG, BRG, employee, or veteran roster.
  • A member form, survey, upload, attendance list, or completion check.
  • A campaign link, tracking parameter, dashboard, or member-usage report.
  • Paid member access, advertising, or the sale of member information.

Employer and member boundary

Through this package, the employer receives no individual member activity from Transition Ops. That includes no individual plan, separation date, service status, checklist, resume, prompt, benefits activity, readiness status, visit history, or completion status.

Use of Transition Ops is optional and remains separate from employer program administration. ERG or BRG participation does not create a right to inspect a member's Transition Ops activity.

Employer-managed systems

If someone opens or shares the link through an employer-managed browser, device, network, intranet, email, or collaboration service, those systems may create ordinary security, access, or communications records. Those records remain governed by the employer's systems, notices, and policies.

Public-app privacy is separate

This employer-package boundary does not describe or certify every data flow in the public Transition Ops app. The public app's providers, privacy practices, notices, and member controls must be reviewed and validated separately.

What the organization controls

  • Its ERG or BRG audience, owner, sponsor, contacts, and publication surface.
  • Its internal benefits, leave, accommodations, employee-relations, and escalation information.
  • Its privacy, legal, accessibility, security, procurement, communications, and brand review.
  • Any mentoring, event, membership, or employee-program process.

What is not part of this package

  • Employer access to individual or group Transition Ops activity.
  • A retention, recruiting, productivity, promotion, hiring, readiness, or ROI claim.
  • An ERG roster, event, budget, communications, case-management, or analytics system.
  • A client-specific page, native app, integration, analytics feed, or co-branding.
  • Legal, medical, financial, tax, command, or benefits advice.

Internal review checklist

  • Identify the military-connected ERG, BRG, veteran network, or program owner.
  • Identify the executive sponsor and final internal approver.
  • Select the existing internal resource area where the direct link would appear.
  • Complete the organization's required privacy, legal, accessibility, security, procurement, communications, and brand reviews.
  • Assign a role-based internal contact for employer-policy questions.
  • Assign an owner and date for refreshing or removing the resource.

Next step

Send this brief to the organization's ERG or BRG owner and existing review team. Do not attach member plans, resumes, prompts, benefits details, medical information, employee records, or other sensitive personal information. This brief provides no VBS contact or automated outreach route.
