Transition Ops for the military-connected community

Transition Ops is a free public planning resource for service members preparing to separate or retire, veterans, Guard and Reserve members, military spouses, caregivers, and families.

The resource brings transition timelines, benefits resources, career tools, and practical checklists into one place. It can be opened directly without an employer account or connection to an employer system.

Use of Transition Ops is optional.

  3. Add exact destination https://transitionops.org.
  4. Link title: Transition Ops.
  5. Description: Free transition-planning resources for service members, veterans, Guard and Reserve members, military spouses, caregivers, and families.
  6. Use an image stored in the organization's approved SharePoint asset library. Do not load it from Transition Ops or another external site.
  7. If a QR code is included, use this supplied static QR containing only https://transitionops.org.
  8. Do not add a redirect, shortened URL, campaign code, tracking parameter, employee identifier, client identifier, referral code, audience targeting, form, survey, or completion requirement.
  9. Place the boundary note immediately below the link or QR.
  10. Check desktop/mobile, keyboard access, alternative text, and normal internal review before publication.

This is an optional external resource. This static package provides only information, a QR code, and a direct link to Transition Ops. It does not include a form, account connection, employee or client identifier, tracking parameter, dashboard, or employer-facing usage report.

Employer-managed devices, browsers, networks, intranets, email, collaboration platforms, and security tools may record access or communications under the employer's own notices and policies.
